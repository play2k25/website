ABOUT_ME.TXT

Anthony Magnani is a Toronto-based writer and educator whose work lives at the intersection of technology, media, and what it means to learn. Trained in Integrated Media at OCAD University, he has spent his career building things that help people see differently — from art installations and makerspace labs to media literacy curricula designed to help young people navigate an AI-saturated world.
